On the day of the conference, sign in to the [PKI Consortium portal](/portal/) with the email address you registered with and open the conference. The event home shows **Watch live** while a streamed session is running, and **Join online** on a session takes you to the live stream of the room it is held in. You can switch rooms, see what is on now and next, and open any session's details without leaving the stream. After the conference, the recordings will be added to this website and our YouTube channel.

When attending the conference virtually, you will have access to all the live-streamed sessions, including keynote speeches and panel discussions. The breakout sessions will not be live-streamed or recorded.
