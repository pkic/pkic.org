## Venue

The conference takes place at **[Meervaart](https://meervaart.nl/en/meetings-events/)**, a modern event venue located between the Amsterdam city center and Schiphol Airport.

**Address:** Meer en Vaart 300, 1068 LE Amsterdam, The Netherlands

## Getting there

### Public transport

Amsterdam public transport (GVB) serves Meervaart directly. Use [9292.nl](https://9292.nl/en/?naar=amsterdam_meer-en-vaart-300) to plan your journey.

- **Tram 1** — stop *Meer en Vaart*
- **Tram 17** — stop *Osdorpplein Oost*
- **Bus 369** — stop *Osdorpplein Oost*

### By car

Use [Google Maps](https://www.google.nl/maps/dir/de+Meervaart+Theater,+Meer+en+Vaart+300,+1068+LE+Amsterdam/@52.358988,4.807397,17z) for directions to Meer en Vaart 300, Amsterdam.

### Parking

**[Parking Garage Nieuw-West](https://parking-nieuwwest.nl/) (recommended)**, a short walk from Meervaart

For December 1–3, use the vehicle entrance at **Nicolaas Anslijnstraat 276, 1068 XA Amsterdam**, on the side of the garage closest to Meervaart. If that entrance is closed, use **Jan Celestraat 11 (under Jumbo)** to enter the same garage. The [garage operator's notice](https://parking-nieuwwest.nl/werkzaamheden-nicolaas-anslijnstraat-bij-parking-nieuw-west/) lists the Nicolaas Anslijnstraat entrance as closed through November 27.

The [conference parking rate](https://meervaart.nl/en/meetings-events/about-meervaart/directions) is €12.50 per day. Pick up a discount ticket at Meervaart reception and keep it with your original parking ticket for when you exit the garage.

**[Q-Park Osdorpplein](https://www.q-park.nl/en-gb/parking/amsterdam/osdorpplein/)**, a 1-minute walk from Meervaart

Osdorpplein 249, 1068 SW Amsterdam. [P1 lists](https://www.p1.nl/parkeerplaats/q-park-osdorpplein/) the first hour as free, then €2.00 per 30 minutes or part thereof.

A designated disabled parking space is available directly in front of the main entrance (not reservable; free with a disabled parking card).
