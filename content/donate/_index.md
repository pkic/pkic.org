The PKI Consortium is a **Utah non-profit** that advances trust in digital communications through open collaboration, education, and community events.

**Membership and our resources are free — and we intend to keep it that way.** There are no dues and no paywalls. Our working groups and publications are open to all.

We keep our conferences **as accessible as we can**. Sponsor funding and voluntary donations make that possible and help us offer free tickets to people who couldn't attend otherwise. This model lets us host events **anywhere in the world** and keeps the focus on the community.

## The real cost of an open event

Keeping membership free ensures that **every practitioner** — regardless of employer size or budget — can participate in working groups, access resources, and shape PKI standards. The moment we charge membership fees, we lose the voices that matter most.

Keeping conferences open and affordable means **anyone can take part** — not just those with a generous employer budget. That openness is what makes our events uniquely valuable: the full spectrum of the PKI community in one room.

But openness is not without cost. A professional event costs **$150–$300 per attendee per day** — venue, A/V, catering, travel support, and logistics. A typical three-day event costs us **$450–$900 per person we welcome through the door**. We rely on sponsor funding and voluntary donations to make it happen — and the more we raise, the more seats we can offer.

Your donation keeps all of this open and growing:

- **Free membership** for all practitioners, regardless of ability to pay
- **Open, affordable conferences** hosted around the world, with free tickets for people who couldn't attend otherwise
- Research and working groups advancing PKI best practices
- Open-source tooling and developer resources

**Without voluntary donations**, we would have to cap event attendance and hold fewer conferences — excluding the very practitioners who benefit most from being here.

## What your gift makes possible

Every amount has a direct use — and forty people giving $50 equals two people giving $1,000. Both matter.

| Amount | What your gift makes possible |
|-------:|-------------------------------|
| **$2,500** | Helps us welcome **3–5 attendees** for a full three-day event — covering venue, A/V, and catering |
| **$1,000** | Funds a working group publication or covers the full cost of **1–2 conference attendees** |
| **$500** | Covers **one attendee for the full event** — the real per-person cost. This is what it takes to welcome one more person |
| **$250** | Supports open-source tooling development, or covers **one full day** of attendance for one person |
| **$100** | Helps keep membership free for practitioners whose employers cannot contribute |
| **$50** | Joins the community effort — many gifts at this level fund a workshop or expand event capacity |
